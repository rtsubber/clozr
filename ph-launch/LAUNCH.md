## Description

**The Clozr** is an AI meeting assistant that turns conversations into proposals.

### How it works:
1. **Record** — Hit the mic button during any meeting (in-person, phone, Zoom)
2. **Transcribe** — AI transcribes with speaker identification in seconds
3. **Propose** — Auto-generates a professional proposal matched to your services

### What makes it different:
- **Proposals, not summaries** — Other AI meeting tools give you notes. Clozr gives you a deliverable you can send to clients.
- **Your catalog, your pricing** — Import your services from your website or upload a PDF. Clozr matches meeting pain points to your actual offerings.
- **30 seconds, not 30 minutes** — From recording to shareable proposal link in the time it takes to say "I'll send that over."
- **Browser-based** — No app download. Works on any device with a mic.
- **Speaker diarization** — Know who said what, powered by Deepgram Nova-3.

### Pricing:
- **Free** — 5 meetings, full features
- **Pro** — $19/mo (unlimited meetings)
- **Business** — $39/mo (team features + priority processing)
- **Self-Hosted** — $79/mo (run on your own server)

### Built with:
- Groq Whisper (speech-to-text in <3 seconds)
- Deepgram Nova-3 (speaker diarization)
- Llama 3.3 70B (proposal generation)
- Stripe (payments)
